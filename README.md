## About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher working at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/profile/todarwal) and [Karolinska Institutet](https://ki.se/en/people/yogesh-todarwal).

My current research focuses on trustworthy and explainable machine learning for healthcare early-warning systems. I am interested in developing computational models that can identify meaningful patterns in complex data while providing explanations that support real-world decision-making.

My broader background is in computational science, scientific machine learning, molecular simulation, quantitative modeling, data analysis, and high-performance computing. During my PhD at KTH, I developed accurate force-field and multiscale simulation methods for studying biomolecular systems, molecular interactions, photophysical properties, and self-assembling materials.
